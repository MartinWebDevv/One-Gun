# ONE GUN DESIGN BIBLE v1.0

## Vision

**One Gun is the game where the strongest player can lose everything in
one swing.**

## Core Pillars

-   Equal footing for every player.
-   Skill over meta.
-   Controlled chaos.
-   Spectacle through skill.
-   Toy Box League identity.

## Toy Box League

Collectible toy mascots compete for the **Golden One Gun Trophy**.

## Visual Identity

-   50% Premium Toy Packaging
-   30% Nintendo Polish
-   20% 90s Arcade Energy

## Gameplay

-   No pay-to-win.
-   No permanent power advantages.
-   Movement, positioning, stamina, aim and decision making determine
    victory.

## Progression

Unlock: - Mascots - Cosmetics - Titles - Badges - Victory poses

Never unlock combat advantages.
